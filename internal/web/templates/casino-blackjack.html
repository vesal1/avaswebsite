House rules, all of them

  • One deck, shuffled fresh every round from the committed seed. No cut card games, no penetration tricks — every hand starts from 52.
  • Dealer stands on all 17s, soft ones included.
  • Blackjack pays 3:2. A 6:5 table is a 1.4% pay cut dressed as a side rule, and we do not run one.
  • Double on any first two cards. No double after a split.
  • Split any pair once. Split aces get one card each; a split 21 is 21, not blackjack.
  • Dealer peeks with an ace or a ten showing, so a double or split is never lost to a dealer natural.
  • No insurance. It is a separate wager returning about 92% dressed up as protection, and we decline to sell it.
