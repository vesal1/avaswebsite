    The ladders, printed

    The multiplier after each safe reveal is exactly 97% of the true odds of surviving that many tiles, floored to four decimal places. The published return for each board is its worst step after that floor — never more than a tenth of a percent under 97%. A run that reaches {{payX10000 .Data.CapX10000}} settles by itself: that is the top of every ladder, and it is a cap on our exposure, not a shave on your steps.
