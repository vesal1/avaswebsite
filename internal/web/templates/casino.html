How the games are decided {{if .User}}My history{{end}}

Every game on this floor is labelled with what it actually is. Skill means your decisions change the return and we print the strategy that maximises it. Your call means the decisions are real but every choice pays the same published rate. Chance means the committed seed decides everything and the return figure is computed from the machine itself. What none of them have is a dial: the engine that settles a round is handed a game, a seed and a stake, and nothing else.
