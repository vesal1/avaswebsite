How the games are decided {{if .User}}My spins{{end}}

Every machine below shows its return to player. That figure is not a target or an average taken from play: it is computed from the reel strips and the paytable, counting every outcome the machine can produce and how often it produces it. The reels themselves are fixed by a seed we commit to before you spin and publish afterwards, so you can replay any spin and check it landed where it was always going to land.
