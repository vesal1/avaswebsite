The algorithm, in full

  1. Commitment is SHA-256(server_seed), published before the deal.
  2. The keystream is HMAC-SHA256(server_seed, client_seed + "|" + nonce + "|" + counter), with a big-endian 8-byte counter starting at zero and incrementing for each further 32-byte block.
  3. The deck starts in order, card 0 to 51, where rank = card / 4 (0 is a two, 12 an ace) and suit = card % 4 (clubs, diamonds, hearts, spades).
  4. Fisher-Yates from the top: for i from 51 down to 1, draw an index in [0, i] and swap.
  5. Indexes are drawn by rejection sampling, not modulo. Read enough whole bytes to cover the range, reject any value at or above floor(2^bits / n) × n, and draw again. A plain modulo would make low-numbered cards fractionally likelier, which is a small bias and not one we are willing to have.
  6. The combined player seed is SHA-256(seed₁ ‖ 0x00 ‖ seed₂ ‖ 0x00 ‖ …) over the seated players in seat order, so no single player can cancel another's.
