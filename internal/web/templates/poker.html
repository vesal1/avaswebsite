How we keep it honest

The deck is decided before the deal, and you can prove it

Before any card is dealt we draw a secret seed and publish only its SHA-256 hash. You and everybody at the table contribute your own seeds, and the shuffle is derived from all of them together. When the hand ends we publish the secret seed. You can check it hashes to what we showed you beforehand, and replay the shuffle to get the exact same deck. We cannot look at the deck and change it, deal a card other than the one the seed dictates, or pick a seed after seeing your cards — all three would break a promise that was already public.

Check a hand yourself

We are paid by the rake, and only the rake

The rake is a fixed percentage, it is capped, and it is shown on every table above before you sit down. A hand that ends before the flop is never raked, so there is nothing to gain from hurrying hands along. Nothing in the software can influence which cards come out.

Cards stay private

Your hole cards are sent to you and to nobody else. A folded hand is never revealed, during the hand or in the history afterwards.
