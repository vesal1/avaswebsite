How wagering is counted

Different products clear a bonus at different rates, because they carry different margins for us. A bonus that cleared at full rate on near-even-money bets would be a promotion to arbitrage rather than one to play, so the rates differ — and they are published here rather than being something you find out afterwards.

  • A bonus that expires before it clears is removed; your own money stays.
  • Staking draws on your own cash first, so a bonus you later give up costs you as little as possible.
  • Once a bonus clears, the remaining balance becomes ordinary withdrawable cash.
