Safer gambling

Betting should be entertainment you can afford. It is not a way to make money, and it is not a way out of money trouble. The tools below are always available and take effect immediately.

Set a limit

You can cap how much you deposit each day, week or month, how much you stake in a day, and how much you can lose in a day or week. Tightening a limit applies at once. Raising one takes 24 hours, and the old limit keeps applying in the meantime, so a limit can never be lifted in the moment it starts to bite.

Set my limits

Take a break

A break of 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days signs you out and stops you betting. You keep access to your wallet, so you can withdraw whatever is left.

Self-exclude

Self-exclusion blocks betting for six months, a year, five years or permanently. It cannot be reversed by you or by us. You will still be able to withdraw your balance.

Reality checks

Every {{.Data.Config.RealityCheckMin}} minutes of play we interrupt you with how long you have been betting and how much you are up or down.

Warning signs

  • Betting more than you planned, or more than you can afford to lose.
  • Chasing losses with bigger stakes.
  • Borrowing money to bet, or hiding betting from people close to you.
  • Betting to escape stress or low mood.

Free, confidential help

Support is available whatever your situation, and it is free. GamCare, Gambling Therapy and Gamblers Anonymous all offer confidential advice, and national gambling helplines operate in most jurisdictions. Free debt advice services can help if betting has caused financial difficulty.

Under-age gambling

You must be at least {{.Data.Config.MinimumAge}} to hold an account. We verify age at registration and identity before any payout. Accounts found to belong to someone under age are closed and stakes returned.
