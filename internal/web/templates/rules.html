Betting rules

The basics

  • All prices are decimal odds. A price of 2.50 returns 2.5 times the stake, the stake included.
  • Balances, stakes and payouts are in bitcoin, counted in satoshi. Fractions of a satoshi are never paid.
  • A bet cannot be cancelled once it is accepted.
  • Minimum stake {{btc .Data.Config.MinStakeSat}} BTC, maximum stake {{btc .Data.Config.MaxStakeSat}} BTC.
  • The most any single bet can return is {{btc .Data.Config.MaxPayoutSat}} BTC.
  • A multiple may carry at most {{.Data.Config.MaxParlayLegs}} selections.

Price changes

Prices move. If a price changes between adding a selection and placing the bet, we refuse the bet and show you the new price, unless you have ticked “accept price changes”. A bet is always struck at the live price, never the one that has already moved.

Voids and refunds

  • An abandoned event voids every market on it, and stakes are returned.
  • A void leg inside a multiple settles at a price of 1.00; the rest of the multiple stands.
  • A withdrawn competitor voids bets on them in outright and matchup markets.
  • If the actual result was never among the prices offered, for example an exact score we did not price, the market is voided rather than settled as a loss.
  • A whole-number handicap or total that lands exactly on the line is a push and the stake is returned.

Multiples

Selections from the same event cannot be combined in a multiple: their outcomes are related, so a combined price would not reflect the real chance of both landing.

Asian lines

Quarter lines such as -0.25 and -0.75 split your stake across the two neighbouring half lines. That is why an Asian bet can return half your stake, or win on one half and push on the other.

Settlement

Markets are settled from the official result. Where a result cannot decide a market on its own, a trader settles it by hand. We would rather settle a market late than settle it wrongly, because a payout cannot be taken back.
